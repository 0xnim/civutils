Nether Quartz Ore generates throughout the Nether and drops Quartz when mined. It's one of the most common ores in the Nether.

**Generation:**
- Found at all Y-levels in the Nether
- Very common - good XP source
- Requires Expert (3) Miner to harvest reliably

Quartz is used for crafting Observers, Daylight Detectors, and decorative Quartz Blocks.
