Obsidian is a hard block formed when water flows over lava source blocks. It requires Master (4) Miner to harvest and a Diamond or Netherite pickaxe.

**Uses:**
- Building Nether Portals (minimum 10 blocks)
- Crafting Enchanting Tables
- Crafting Ender Chests
- Blast-resistant building material

**Mining Tips:**
- Takes 9.4 seconds with a Diamond Pickaxe
- Takes 8.35 seconds with a Netherite Pickaxe
- Efficiency V reduces mining time significantly
- Create obsidian farms by placing water over lava pools
