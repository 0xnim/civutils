Gold Ore has very limited distribution, only generating in hot climate biomes. Best Y-level for mining is Y -16.

**Generation Rates:**
- **No Gold**: Most biomes (Temperate, Warm, Cold, Polar, Beach, Ocean, Caves, Mushroom Island, Cherry Forest, Volcano)
- Tier 2 Full (11 veins/chunk): Hot Flatland, Hot Forest
- Tier 2+4 (12 veins/chunk): Desert, Hot Mountain (with Large Veins)

**Large Gold Ore Veins:**
- Y-Range: 32 to 96
- Coverage: 7% (uncommon)
- Indicator Block: **Granite**
- Contains: Gold Ore + Deepslate Gold Ore + **Raw Gold Blocks**
- Spawns in: Desert, Hot Mountain

For large veins, best Y-level is Y 64. Look for Granite formations in Desert or Hot Mountain biomes.
