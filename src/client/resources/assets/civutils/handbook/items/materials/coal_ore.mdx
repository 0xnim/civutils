Coal Ore is the most common ore, found between Y 0 and Y 100 in most biomes. Best Y-level for mining is Y 50.

**Generation Rates:**
- Tier 3 (24 veins/chunk): Most biomes including Flatlands, Forests, Mountains, Desert, Beach, Ocean, Mushroom Island, Cherry Forest
- Tier 4 (38 veins/chunk): Lush Cave, Cave (with Large Veins)
- Tier 5 (45 veins/chunk): Volcano (with Large Veins)

**Large Coal Ore Veins:**
- Y-Range: 0 to 250
- Coverage: 10% (very common)
- Indicator Block: **Andesite**
- Spawns in: Volcano, Lush Cave, Cave

When you see Andesite formations between Y 0-250 in these biomes, mine into them to find coal ore deposits.
