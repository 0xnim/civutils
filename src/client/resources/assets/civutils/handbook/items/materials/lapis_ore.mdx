Lapis Lazuli Ore has very limited distribution, only generating in warm climate biomes. Best Y-level for mining is Y 0.

**Generation Rates:**
- **No Lapis**: Most biomes (Temperate, Hot, Cold, Polar, Desert, Beach, Ocean, Caves, Mushroom Island, Cherry Forest, Volcano)
- Tier 2 (8 veins/chunk): Warm Flatland, Warm Forest
- Tier 4 (30 veins/chunk): Warm Mountain (with Large Veins)

**Large Lapis Ore Veins:**
- Y-Range: -32 to 32
- Coverage: 4.5%
- Indicator Block: **Calcite**
- Spawns in: Warm Mountain only

Look for Calcite formations in Warm Mountain biomes between Y -32 and Y 32.
