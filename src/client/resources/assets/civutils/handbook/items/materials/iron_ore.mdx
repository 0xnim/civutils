Iron Ore is one of the most important resources. It generates across multiple height bands with concentration peaking around Y 16.

**Generation Rates:**
- Tier 2 (43 veins/chunk): Flatlands, Forests, Desert, Beach, Ocean, Mushroom Island, Volcano
- Tier 3 (59 veins/chunk): All Mountains, Polar, Lush Cave, Cave

**Large Iron Ore Veins:**
- Y-Range: -50 to 0
- Coverage: 9% (common)
- Indicator Block: **Tuff**
- Contains: Deepslate Iron Ore + Raw Iron Blocks
- Spawns in: **Beach only**

When mining in Beach biomes, look for Tuff formations between Y -50 to 0. These indicate large iron deposits that may contain Raw Iron Blocks.
