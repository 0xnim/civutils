Deepslate is a dense stone variant found below Y 0. While it requires Apprentice (1) Miner to harvest, it still grants 2 XP per block even when you fail to collect it - making it an excellent early leveling resource.

**Generation:**
- Generates below Y 0
- Gradually replaces Stone between Y 0 and Y -8
- All ores in this layer become Deepslate variants

**Leveling Strategy:**
Strip mining through Deepslate at low Y-levels is one of the most efficient ways to gain Miner XP early on. Even at Novice (0), you gain XP from breaking it - you just won't collect the block.

Deepslate takes longer to mine than regular Stone, so bring extra pickaxes.
