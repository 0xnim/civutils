Calcite is a white mineral block found in Amethyst Geodes and as an indicator block for Large Lapis Veins.

**Generation:**
- Part of Amethyst Geode structure (middle layer)
- Indicator block for Large Lapis Veins in Warm Mountain biomes

**Uses:**
- Decorative building block
- Indicates nearby Lapis deposits when found in mountains

When you see Calcite formations in Warm Mountain biomes between Y -32 and Y 32, mine into them to find Large Lapis Veins.
