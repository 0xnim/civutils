Emerald Ore has very limited but potentially abundant generation in cold climate biomes. Best Y-level for mining is Y 64-120.

**Generation Rates:**
- **No Emerald**: Most biomes (Temperate, Warm, Hot, Desert, Beach, Ocean, Caves, Mushroom Island, Cherry Forest, Volcano)
- Tier 2 (4 veins/chunk): Cold Flatland, Cold Forest
- Tier 4 (303 veins/chunk!): Cold Mountain, Polar (with Large Veins)

**Large Emerald Ore Veins:**
- Y-Range: 0 to 200
- Coverage: 3.5%
- Indicator Block: **Diorite**
- Spawns in: Cold Mountain, Polar

Cold Mountain and Polar biomes have the highest emerald concentration in the game at 303 veins per chunk! Look for Diorite formations at higher elevations.
