Redstone Ore has very limited distribution, only generating in temperate climate biomes. Best Y-level for mining is Y -56.

**Generation Rates:**
- **No Redstone**: Most biomes (Warm, Hot, Cold, Polar, Desert, Beach, Ocean, Caves, Mushroom Island, Cherry Forest, Volcano)
- Tier 2 (23 veins/chunk): Temperate Flatland, Temperate Forest
- Tier 4 (33 veins/chunk): Temperate Mountain (with Large Veins)

**Large Redstone Ore Veins:**
- Y-Range: -64 to 16
- Coverage: 5.5%
- Indicator Block: **Blackstone**
- Contains: Deepslate Redstone Ore
- Spawns in: Temperate Mountain only

Look for Blackstone formations in Temperate Mountain biomes between Y -64 and Y 16.
