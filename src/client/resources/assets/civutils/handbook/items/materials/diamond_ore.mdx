Diamond Ore is the most valuable standard ore. It generates in three height bands with concentration highest in the lowest band (Y -64 to -44). Best Y-level for mining is Y -54 to -59.

**Generation Rates:**
- Tier 1 (6 veins/chunk): Flatlands, Forests, Beach, Ocean, Caves, Mushroom Island
- Tier 2 (9 veins/chunk): All Mountains, Desert, Polar, Cherry Forest, Volcano

**Large Diamond Ore Veins:**
- Y-Range: -64 to 100
- Coverage: 2% (extremely rare)
- Indicator Block: **Obsidian**
- Spawns in: **Volcano only**

Volcano biome is the best for diamond mining - it has Tier 2 rates AND is the only biome with large diamond veins. Look for Obsidian formations underground.
