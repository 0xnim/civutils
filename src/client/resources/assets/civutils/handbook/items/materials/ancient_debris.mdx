Ancient Debris is the rarest ore in the game, found only in the Nether. It requires Grandmaster (5) Miner to harvest and gives 100 XP per block - the highest of any ore.

**Generation:**
- Y-Range: 8 to 22 (concentrated around Y 15)
- Very rare: typically 1-2 veins per chunk
- Blast resistant - TNT mining is effective

**Processing:**
Ancient Debris must be smelted into Netherite Scrap, then combined with Gold Ingots at a crafting table to create Netherite Ingots.
