Nether Gold Ore generates throughout the Nether and drops Gold Nuggets when mined. It's more common than Overworld gold but yields less gold per ore.

**Generation:**
- Found at all Y-levels in the Nether
- More common than Ancient Debris
- Requires Expert (3) Miner to harvest reliably

Piglins become hostile if you mine this ore near them unless you're wearing gold armor.
