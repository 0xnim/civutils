Blueprints are created by Librarians and required by Blacksmiths to craft iron tier and above equipment. Each item type requires its own specific blueprint. The tool head (like [Iron Pickaxe Head](item:iron_pickaxe_head)) or plate set (like [Iron Helmet Set](item:iron_helmet_set)) used in the recipe is **NOT consumed**.

See the [Smithing System](smithing) guide for full details on the crafting process.
