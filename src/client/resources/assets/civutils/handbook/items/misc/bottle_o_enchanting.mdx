Bottle o' Enchanting is created by Librarians at Expert level (3) by shift right-clicking a water bottle. This consumes 6 hunger and 4 Librarian XP. When thrown, the bottle breaks and releases experience orbs that grant vanilla Minecraft XP (not Class XP).

The bottle's lore displays who blessed it. This is useful for transferring Minecraft experience to other players who need it for enchanting or other level-based activities.

**Note:** This is different from [Liquid XP Bottles](item:liquid_xp_bottle) which are drinkable and grant Class XP through the Emerald Infusion system.
