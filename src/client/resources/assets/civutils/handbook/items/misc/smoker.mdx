The smoker is a specialized furnace that cooks food twice as fast as a regular furnace. More importantly, food cooked in a smoker becomes **smoked food**, which lasts longer before [expiring](food-expiration).

Requires Farmer level 1 to interact with.

## Benefits of Smoked Food

- Lasts significantly longer than regular cooked food
- Same hunger and saturation values
- Essential for long expeditions or storage

## Tips

- Always use smokers instead of furnaces for food
- Pair with a [Farmer](farmer) to maximize food production
- Smoked food stacks normally with regular cooked food
