The composter converts plant materials and food into bone meal. This is invaluable for farmers who need bone meal to speed up crop growth.

Requires Farmer level 1 to interact with.

## How It Works

1. Right-click the composter with compostable items (seeds, crops, leaves, etc.)
2. The composter fills up layer by layer
3. When full, it produces bone meal

| Item | Fill Chance |
|------|-------------|
| Pumpkin Pie | 100% |

| Baked Potato | 85% |
| Cookies | 85% |

| Melon/Pumpkin | 65% |
| Carrots/Potatoes | 65% |
| Wheat | 65% |
| Seeds | 30% |

## Tips

- Use excess seeds and low-value crops for composting
- Bone meal gives 5 XP per use for Farmers
- Essential for early-game crop production
