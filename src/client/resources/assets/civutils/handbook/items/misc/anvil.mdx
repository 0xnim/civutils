The anvil is used to repair items, combine enchantments, and rename items. It's essential for maintaining high-level equipment.

Requires Blacksmith level 2 to craft and level 3 to use.

## Uses

### Repairing Items
- Combine a damaged item with materials to restore durability
- Costs Minecraft XP levels
- More efficient with higher Blacksmith level

### Combining Enchantments
- Combine two enchanted items to merge their enchantments
- The target item keeps all compatible enchantments from both
- Useful for creating powerful equipment

### Renaming Items
- Give items custom names
- Named items are easier to identify in inventories

## Damage

Anvils take damage with use and progress through stages:
1. Anvil (new)
2. Chipped Anvil
3. Damaged Anvil
4. Destroyed (drops nothing)

Each use has a 12% chance to damage the anvil.

## Tips

- Save anvil uses for important operations
- Blacksmiths can also repair items by shift right-clicking the anvil with an item
- Use a [Whetstone](item:whetstone) to restore stat decay on equipment
