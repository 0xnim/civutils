The stonecutter allows precise cutting of stone blocks into stairs, slabs, walls, and decorative variants. It's more efficient than crafting for many stone recipes.

Requires Builder level 1 to interact with.

## Benefits Over Crafting

| Recipe | Crafting | Stonecutter |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Stairs | 6 blocks → 4 stairs | 1 block → 1 stair |
| Slabs | 3 blocks → 6 slabs | 1 block → 2 slabs |
| Walls | 6 blocks → 6 walls | 1 block → 1 wall |

## Supported Materials

- Stone / Cobblestone / Mossy Cobblestone
- Stone Bricks / Mossy Stone Bricks
- Deepslate / Cobbled Deepslate
- Granite / Diorite / Andesite
- Sandstone / Red Sandstone
- Prismarine / Quartz
- Blackstone / Basalt
- Tuff / Calcite
- And more...

## Tips

- Always use stonecutter for stairs to save materials
- Place near your building site for convenience
- Pairs well with [Builder](builder) crafting unlocks
