# Whetstone

The Whetstone is a maintenance tool used by [Blacksmiths](blacksmith) to restore decayed stats on tools and armor.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Notes |
|------------|-------|
| Stone-based materials | Exact recipe varies |

**Required Level:** [Blacksmith](blacksmith)

## The Durability System

In CivLabs, equipment has an **attribute decay** system:

### How Decay Works
1. Tools and armor have base stats (damage, protection, etc.)
2. As durability decreases, **stats also decrease**
3. A heavily-used tool deals less damage than a fresh one
4. Armor provides less protection as it wears down

### Whetstone Effect
- **Temporarily restores** stats to full
- Does NOT repair durability
- Effect is temporary and decays again with use

## Usage

### For Blacksmiths
1. Hold the tool or armor to maintain
2. Use whetstone on it
3. Stats are restored

### When to Use
- Before important combat
- Before difficult mining expeditions
- When you notice reduced effectiveness

## Related Systems

### Waxing
Tools and armor can be **waxed** to make them last longer:
- Slows durability loss
- Extends time between maintenance

### Material Return
When equipment finally breaks:
- Returns some materials
- Partial recovery of investment

## Tips

1. **Pre-Combat:** Sharpen weapons before fights
2. **Track Performance:** Notice when tools feel weaker
3. **Don't Wait:** Maintain equipment regularly
4. **Combine with Waxing:** Wax + Whetstone = longer-lasting gear

## Strategic Value

In a system where death resets class progress, maintaining equipment is crucial:
- Better stats = better survival
- Better survival = keeping your progress
- Regular maintenance is worth the effort
