# Plate Stacks

Plate Stacks are [Metal Plates](metal-plates) combined together, used as the primary material for crafting [Plate Sets](plate-sets) (armor) and [Tool Heads](tool-heads).

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| [Metal Plates](metal-plates) | Multiple |

**Crafting:** Stack plates together in a specific pattern
**Required Level:** [Blacksmith](blacksmith) varies by tier

## Plate Stack Tiers

| Stack Type | Source | Blacksmith Level |
|------------|--------|------------------|
| Copper Plate Stack | Copper Plates | Apprentice (1) |
| Iron Plate Stack | Iron Plates | Journeyman (2) |
| Gold Plate Stack | Gold Plates | Expert (3) |
| Diamond Plate Stack | Diamond Plates | Master (4) |
| Netherite Plate Stack | Netherite Plates | Grandmaster (5) |

## Uses

### Armor ([Plate Sets](plate-sets))
Arrange plate stacks in armor shapes:
- Helmet shape
- Chestplate shape
- Leggings shape
- Boots shape

### Tools ([Tool Heads](tool-heads))
Arrange plate stacks in tool patterns:

| Tool | Plate Stacks |
|------|--------------|
| Shovel | 1 |
| Hoe | 2 (horizontal) |
| Sword | 2 (vertical) |
| Pickaxe | 3 (pickaxe shape) |
| Axe | 3 (axe shape) |

## Crafting Chain

```
Metal Ingot → Metal Plate → Plate Stack → Plate Set/Tool Head → Finished Item
```

## Material Requirements

### Per Plate Stack
The exact number of plates per stack varies. Check in-game recipes.

### Planning
Calculate backwards from what you need:
1. Determine final items needed
2. Calculate plate sets/tool heads required
3. Calculate plate stacks needed
4. Calculate plates needed
5. Calculate ingots needed

## Tips

1. **Batch Craft:** Make all stacks before moving to next step
2. **Stay Organized:** Keep different tiers separate
3. **Check Recipes:** Verify exact amounts in-game
4. **[Blacksmith](blacksmith) Only:** Only Blacksmiths can craft these
