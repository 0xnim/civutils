# Blaze Rod

In CivLabs, Blaze Rods can be **crafted** by high-level [Healers](healer), enabling potion brewing without needing to find a Nether Fortress.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Warped Nylium | 1 |
| Crimson Nylium | 1 |
| Gold Ingot | 1 |
| Gunpowder | 3 |

**Required Level:** [Healer](healer) Master (4)

## Uses

### Blaze Powder
Blaze Rods can be crafted into Blaze Powder:
- 1 Blaze Rod → 2 Blaze Powder

### Brewing Stand Fuel
Blaze Powder is required to fuel Brewing Stands.

### Nether Wart Recipe
Blaze Powder is used in crafting [Nether Wart](nether-wart):
- Beetroot + 3 Coarse Dirt + Blaze Powder

## XP Gain

Crafting Blaze Rods gives **45 Healer CXP**.

## Obtaining Ingredients

### Warped & Crimson Nylium
- Found in the Nether
- Warped: Warped Forest biome
- Crimson: Crimson Forest biome

### Gold Ingot
- [Miners](miner) at Expert (3) can mine gold
- [Blacksmiths](blacksmith) smelt gold ore

### Gunpowder
- Dropped by Creepers
- [Guardsmen](guardsman) can farm these

## Potion Brewing Path

```
Blaze Rod (this)
    ↓
Blaze Powder (fuel + ingredient)
    ↓
Nether Wart (Healer Level 5)
    ↓
Brewing Stand (Healer Level 4)
    ↓
Potions!
```

## Tips

1. **Nether Expedition:** Need nylium from the Nether
2. **Coordinate:** Need Miners (gold), Guardsmen (gunpowder)
3. **Worth the Effort:** Potions are powerful late-game
4. **Batch Craft:** Make multiple rods at once

## Strategic Value

Potions provide significant combat advantages:
- Healing potions for emergencies
- Strength/Speed for combat
- Fire resistance for Nether travel
