# Chainmail Plate

Chainmail Plates are used to craft chainmail armor, which is now craftable in CivLabs. Chainmail is a required stepping stone in the armor progression.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Iron Nuggets | Multiple |
| [Padded Leather](padded-leather) | 1 |

**Crafting:** Iron nuggets arranged around padded leather
**Required Level:** [Blacksmith](blacksmith) Apprentice (1)

## Uses

Chainmail Plates are used in standard armor shapes to create chainmail armor:

| Piece | Chainmail Plates |
|-------|------------------|
| Helmet | 5 |
| Chestplate | 8 |
| Leggings | 7 |
| Boots | 4 |
| **Total Set** | **24** |

## Armor Progression

Chainmail armor is **required** as the base for iron armor:

```
Leather Armor
    ↓
Chainmail Armor (this)
    ↓
Iron Armor
    ↓
Gold Armor
    ↓
Diamond Armor
    ↓
Netherite Armor
```

You cannot skip chainmail in the progression.

## Material Requirements

### Per Full Chainmail Set
| Material | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Chainmail Plates | 24 |
| [Padded Leather](padded-leather) | 24 |
| Iron Nuggets | Varies by recipe |
| Leather | 24 |
| Wool | 24 |

### Iron Nugget Source
- Smelt iron tools/armor for nuggets
- Or craft: 1 Iron Ingot → 9 Iron Nuggets

## Crafting Tips

1. **Save Iron:** Nuggets stretch your iron supply
2. **Prepare Leather:** Need lots of padded leather
3. **Batch Craft:** Make all plates before armor
4. **[Blacksmith](blacksmith) Only:** Only Blacksmiths can craft these

## Strategic Value

Chainmail provides:
- Better protection than leather
- Required base for iron armor
- Relatively affordable iron cost
