# Bandage

Bandages are the primary healing item in CivLabs, used by [Healers](healer) to heal and revive other players. Only Healers can craft and effectively use them.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Paper | 8 |
| Sugarcane | 1 |

**Crafting:** Paper surrounding sugarcane in center
**Required Level:** Healer Apprentice (1)

## Costs

| Action | Hunger Cost |
|--------|-------------|
| Crafting | 2 hunger |
| Using | 5 hunger |

## Healing Amount

Healing scales with Healer level:

| Level | Healing | Bandaging XP |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| Apprentice (1) | Base | 15 |
| Journeyman (2) | Improved | 23 |
| Expert (3) | Better | 32 |
| Master (4) | Strong | 41 |
| Grandmaster (5) | Maximum | 50 |

## Uses

### Self-Healing
Use the bandage directly to heal yourself.

### Healing Others
Right-click on another player to heal them.

### Revival
When used on a **downed player**, initiates the revival minigame:

1. A chest UI opens with red and green colored items
2. Click red items to turn them green
3. Clicking green items turns them red and adds more reds
4. When all items are green, the player is revived

## Crafting Tips

### Paper Production
- 3 Sugarcane → 3 Paper
- Crafting paper gives 9 Healer XP

### Sugarcane Farming
- Need ~4 stacks of sugarcane to reach Healer Level 1
- Work with [Farmers](farmer) for reliable harvests

## Strategy

1. **Prioritize Sugarcane:** You need lots early on
2. **Manage Hunger:** Both crafting and using cost hunger
3. **Stay Safe:** Healers are critical - don't risk yourself
4. **Stockpile:** Always keep spare bandages ready
