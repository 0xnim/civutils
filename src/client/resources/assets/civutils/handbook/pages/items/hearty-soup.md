# Hearty Soup

Hearty Soup is the only healing item that **anyone can craft**, making it essential for early-game survival before a [Healer](healer) reaches Level 1.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Fermented Spider Eye | 1 |
| Bowl | 1 |

**Crafting:** Shapeless recipe

## Effects

- **Heals 2 hearts** instantly
- Grants **50 Healer CXP** when crafted

## Obtaining Ingredients

### Fermented Spider Eye
Standard vanilla recipe:
- 1 Spider Eye + 1 Brown Mushroom + 1 Sugar

### Brown Mushrooms
- Found in dark areas, caves, swamps
- Can spread in dark conditions on podzol

## Usage Tips

1. **Early Healer Leveling:** Craft these to gain Healer XP before bandages
2. **Emergency Healing:** Keep some stocked for urgent situations
3. **Anyone Can Help:** Non-healers can craft these to support the group

## Comparison to Other Healing

| Item | Healing | Requirement |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Hearty Soup | 2 hearts | None |
| [Bandage](bandage) | Scales with level | Healer Level 1 |
| [Blessed Food](blessed-food) | Regen + Absorption | Healer Level 2 |

## Strategy

In the very early game, having someone focus on making Hearty Soup can provide critical healing before your Healer establishes bandage production. The 50 CXP per craft also helps level your Healer faster.
