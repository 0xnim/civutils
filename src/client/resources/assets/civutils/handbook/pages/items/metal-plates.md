# Metal Plates

Metal Plates are the first step in crafting plated armor and metal tools in the [Smithing System](smithing). They are created by smelting metal ingots.

## Recipe

| Input | Output |
|-------|--------|
| Metal Ingot | Metal Plate |

**Process:** Smelt in a furnace or blast furnace
**Required Level:** [Blacksmith](blacksmith) varies by tier

## Plate Tiers

| Plate Type | Source | Blacksmith Level |
|------------|--------|------------------|
| Copper Plate | Copper Ingot | Apprentice (1) |
| Iron Plate | Iron Ingot | Journeyman (2) |
| Gold Plate | Gold Ingot | Expert (3) |
| Diamond Plate | Diamond | Master (4) |
| Netherite Plate | Netherite Ingot | Grandmaster (5) |

## Uses

Metal Plates are combined into [Plate Stacks](plate-stacks), which are then used for:
- [Plate Sets](plate-sets) (armor)
- [Tool Heads](tool-heads) (tools)

## Crafting Chain

```
Metal Ingot
    ↓ (smelt)
Metal Plate
    ↓ (combine)
Plate Stack
    ↓ (shape)
Plate Set / Tool Head
    ↓ (smithing table)
Finished Armor / Tool
```

## Smelting XP

Smelting ores gives [Blacksmith](blacksmith) XP:

| Ore | XP |
|-----|-----|
| Copper Ore | 3 |
| Iron Ore | 6 |
| Gold Ore | 8 |
| Ancient Debris | 100 |

**Note:** Smelting ingots into plates does not give additional XP.

## Tips

1. **Batch Smelt:** Process all your ingots at once
2. **Use Blast Furnace:** Faster smelting for ores
3. **Plan Ahead:** Calculate how many plates you need
4. **Prioritize:** Focus on the tier your Blacksmith can use
