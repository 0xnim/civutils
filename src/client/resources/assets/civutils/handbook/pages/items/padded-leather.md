# Padded Leather

Padded Leather is a fundamental crafting material in the [Smithing System](smithing). It replaces regular leather in armor recipes and is a component of [Tool Handles](tool-handle).

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Leather | 1 |
| Wool | 1 |

**Crafting:** Shapeless recipe
**Required Level:** None (anyone can craft)

## Uses

### Leather Armor
All leather armor now uses Padded Leather instead of regular leather:
- Leather Cap → Padded Leather Cap
- Leather Tunic → Padded Leather Tunic
- Leather Pants → Padded Leather Pants
- Leather Boots → Padded Leather Boots

Use the same armor shapes, just with Padded Leather.

### Tool Handles
Padded Leather is required for [Tool Handles](tool-handle):
- 1 Stick + 2 String + 2 Padded Leather

### Chainmail Plates
Used in [Chainmail Plate](chainmail-plate) recipe:
- Iron Nuggets + Padded Leather

## Material Requirements

### Per Set of Leather Armor
| Piece | Padded Leather |
|-------|----------------|
| Cap | 5 |
| Tunic | 8 |
| Pants | 7 |
| Boots | 4 |
| **Total** | **24** |

This means you need **24 leather** and **24 wool** for one full set.

### Per Tool Handle
- 2 Padded Leather per handle
- One handle per tool

## Obtaining Materials

### Leather
- Kill cows (work with [Farmers](farmer) for breeding)
- Major bottleneck in armor progression

### Wool
- Shear sheep (non-lethal, renewable)
- Kill sheep (less efficient)

## Importance in Progression

Padded Leather is required at every stage of armor:
1. **Leather Armor** - Direct use
2. **Chainmail Armor** - Via chainmail plates
3. **Iron+ Armor** - Chainmail is required as base

This makes leather and wool critical resources from the start.

## Tips

1. **Start Cow Breeding Early:** Leather is always a bottleneck
2. **Don't Kill Sheep:** Shear them for renewable wool
3. **Stockpile:** Keep extra padded leather ready
4. **Coordinate:** [Farmers](farmer) should prioritize cows and sheep
