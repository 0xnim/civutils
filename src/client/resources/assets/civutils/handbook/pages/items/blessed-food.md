# Blessed Food

Blessed Food is regular food that has been enhanced by a [Healer](healer) to provide regeneration and absorption effects when eaten.

## How to Bless Food

**Required Level:** Healer Journeyman (2)

1. Hold food in your hand
2. **Shift right-click** to bless it
3. Costs **10 hunger**

## Effects by Healer Level

| Level | Regeneration | Absorption |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| Journeyman (2) | 5 seconds | 5 seconds |
| Expert (3) | 10 seconds | 10 seconds |
| Master (4) | 15 seconds | 15 seconds |
| Grandmaster (5) | 20 seconds | 20 seconds |

All effects are Level 1 (Regeneration I, Absorption I).

## Additional Benefits

### Extended Expiration
Blessed food has a longer [expiration time](food-expiration) than regular food. This stacks with other preservation methods.

### Healer XP
Blessing food grants **3 Healer CXP** per item blessed.

## Best Foods to Bless

High-saturation foods benefit most from blessing:

| Food | Saturation | Priority |
|------|------------|----------|
| Steak | High | Best |
| Cooked Porkchop | High | Best |
| Golden Carrot | Very High | Excellent |
| Cooked Salmon | Medium | Good |
| Bread | Medium | Okay |

## Distribution Strategy

### Priority Recipients
1. **[Guardsmen](guardsman)** - In combat frequently
2. **[Miners](miner)** - Face danger underground
3. **Anyone on dangerous missions**

### Stockpiling
- Keep unblessed food as backup
- Bless food just before it's needed to maximize shelf life

## Expired Blessed Food

If blessed food expires and is eaten:
- Chance to inflict **Wither** (instead of Poison like regular expired food)
- More dangerous than regular expired food

## Tips

1. **Bless High-Value Food:** Don't waste blessing on low-saturation items
2. **Combine Preservation:** Smoke → Bless → Ice barrel for maximum longevity
3. **Track Expiration:** Use blessed food before it expires
4. **Manage Hunger:** Blessing costs 10 hunger per item
