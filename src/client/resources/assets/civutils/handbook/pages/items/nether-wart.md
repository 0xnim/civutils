# Nether Wart

In CivLabs, Nether Wart can be **crafted** by top-level [Healers](healer), completing the potion brewing capability.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Beetroot | 1 |
| Coarse Dirt | 3 |
| Blaze Powder | 1 |

**Required Level:** [Healer](healer) Grandmaster (5)

## Uses

### Awkward Potion
Nether Wart + Water Bottle = Awkward Potion

Awkward Potion is the base for most other potions.

### Farming
Once crafted, Nether Wart can be planted on Soul Sand and farmed normally.

## XP Gain

Crafting Nether Wart gives **90 Healer CXP**.

## Obtaining Ingredients

### Beetroot
- **Critical ingredient** - cannot be crafted
- Found in village farms or as dungeon loot
- May require exploration or trading

### Coarse Dirt
- Crafted: 2 Dirt + 2 Gravel
- Found naturally in some biomes
- Anyone can gather

### Blaze Powder
- From [Blaze Rods](blaze-rod)
- Requires Healer Level 4 first

## Potion Brewing Chain

```
Healer Level 4: Blaze Rod → Blaze Powder
    ↓
Healer Level 4: Brewing Stand
    ↓
Healer Level 5: Nether Wart (this)
    ↓
Full Potion Brewing Capability
```

## The Beetroot Problem

Beetroot is the major bottleneck:
- Cannot be crafted
- Must find seeds naturally
- Village farms (rare)
- Dungeon chests (rare)

### Solutions
- Scout for villages
- Explore dungeons/mineshafts
- Trade with other groups who found some
- [Farmers](farmer) can grow once seeds are obtained

## Tips

1. **Find Beetroot First:** Plan expeditions specifically for this
2. **Protect Seeds:** Once found, guard them carefully
3. **Farm It:** Set up sustainable beetroot farming
4. **Late Game:** This is an end-game capability

## Strategic Impact

With Nether Wart, your civilization can brew:
- Healing Potions
- Strength Potions
- Speed Potions
- Fire Resistance
- And more...

This is a major strategic advantage.
