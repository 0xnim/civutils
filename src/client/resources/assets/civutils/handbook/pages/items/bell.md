# Bell

In CivLabs, Bells can be crafted instead of only being found in villages.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Gold Ingots | Multiple |
| Plank | 1 |

**Crafting:** Bell shape with gold and plank
**Required Level:** None (anyone can craft)

## Uses

### Ringing
Right-click to ring the bell. The sound travels far and alerts nearby players.

### Raid Mechanic
In vanilla, bells reveal raid mobs with Glowing effect. Functionality in CivLabs may vary.

### Signaling
- Alert teammates to danger
- Call players to meetings
- Mark important locations

### Decoration
- Town centers
- Meeting halls
- Warning posts

## Obtaining Gold

Gold is obtained through:
- [Miners](miner) at Expert (3) can mine gold ore
- [Blacksmiths](blacksmith) smelt gold ore

## Strategic Uses

### Base Defense
- Place at entrances
- Ring to alert defenders
- Create alarm system

### Communication
- Establish bell signals
- "1 ring = all clear"
- "3 rings = danger"

### Town Building
- Central meeting point
- Symbolic importance
- Morale booster

## Tips

1. **Place Strategically:** At high-traffic areas
2. **Establish Signals:** Agree on what rings mean
3. **Protect It:** Bells can be stolen
4. **Multiple Bells:** Different locations, different purposes
