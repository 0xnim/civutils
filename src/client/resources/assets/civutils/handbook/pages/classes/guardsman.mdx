# Guardsman

The Guardsman's main role is to use advanced combat skills to protect other players from hostile mobs and enemy players. They gain permanent combat bonuses and unique security abilities.

## Primary Abilities

### Passive Combat Bonuses
- Permanent strength bonus
- Increased mob aggro (mobs target you over others)
- **50% damage reduction from mobs** (Level 1+)
- Extra permanent hearts as you level up

### Awakened Saving Throw
When damage would bring you to 2 or less hearts, there's a chance to trigger "Awakened":

> *"Awakened: Muscle memory floods back from battles never fought."*

| Level | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Apprentice (1) | Absorption 2 |
| Journeyman (2) | Absorption 2 |
| Expert (3) | Absorption 3 |

### Pat Down
At level 1, shift right-click other players to inspect their inventory. You can't take items, but can see what they're carrying. The target is notified in chat.

### Force Feed
Right-click a player with food to feed them directly.

### Dog Taming
Guardsmen can tame wolves into dogs for combat assistance. Note that **breeding dogs requires a [Farmer](farmer)**, so building a dog army requires class coordination. See [Animal Husbandry](animal-husbandry) for details.

### Player Leashing
At level 5, you can leash downed players using leads. Leashed players cannot move, attack, or break blocks, and can be dragged around.

## Gaining XP

XP is gained per **2 attack damage** (1 full heart) dealt to **hostile mobs**:

- Stronger weapons = more damage = more XP
- Critical hits = more XP
- Only hostile mobs count (not passive animals)

## Class Interactions

- **[Healers](healer)** are essential - combat without healing is very dangerous
- **[Blacksmiths](blacksmith)** provide weapons and armor
- **[Farmers](farmer)** benefit from mob hunting (meat drops)
- **[Miners](miner)** need protection during cave expeditions
