# Healer

The Healer's main role is providing healing to other players. With **natural regeneration disabled**, Healers are the only source of healing through bandages and blessed food. They can also revive downed players and eventually brew potions.

## Primary Abilities

### Hearty Soup
Anyone can craft Hearty Soup (fermented spider eye + bowl) for early healing and Healer XP. Heals 2 hearts.

### Bandages
Level 1+ Healers can craft and use bandages to heal or revive players.
- **Recipe:** 8 paper + 1 sugarcane
- **Cost:** 2 hunger to craft, 5 hunger to use
- Healing amount increases with Healer level

### Revival
When using a bandage on a downed player, a minigame opens with red and green items. Click red items to turn them green. When all are green, the player is revived.

### Blessed Food
Level 2+ Healers can bless food by shift right-clicking while holding it. Costs 10 hunger.

| Level | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Journeyman (2) | 5s Regen 1, 5s Absorption 1 |
| Expert (3) | 10s Regen 1, 10s Absorption 1 |
| Master (4) | 15s Regen 1, 15s Absorption 1 |
| Grandmaster (5) | 20s Regen 1, 20s Absorption 1 |

Blessed food also lasts longer before [expiring](food-expiration).

### Force Feed
Level 3+ Healers can right-click players with food to feed them directly. Gives half the food value (minimum half a shank).

### Pick-Up
Level 3+ Healers can pick up downed players by right-clicking them (without bandages). Shift-click to open their inventory.

### Potion Brewing
High-level Healers can craft brewing ingredients:

**Blaze Rods (Level 4):**
- Warped Nylium + Crimson Nylium + Gold Ingot + 3 Gunpowder

**Nether Wart (Level 5):**
- Beetroot + 3 Coarse Dirt + Blaze Powder

## Gaining XP

| Action | XP | Level Required |
|--------|-----|----------------|
| Crafting Hearty Soup | 50 | Novice (0) |
| Crafting Paper | 9 | Novice (0) |
| Crafting Bandages | 3 | Apprentice (1) |
| Blessing Food | 3 | Journeyman (2) |
| Crafting Blaze Rods | 45 | Master (4) |
| Crafting Nether Wart | 90 | Grandmaster (5) |
| Bandaging Player | 15-50 (scales) | Apprentice (1) |

## Class Interactions

- **[Farmers](farmer)** provide sugarcane and food for healing items
- **[Librarians](librarian)** need paper (Healers craft it for XP)
- **[Guardsmen](guardsman)** need constant healing support in combat

## Tips

1. **Get Sugarcane First:** You need ~4 stacks to reach level 1
2. **Consider Farmer First:** Level Farmer to gather sugarcane reliably, then death reset
3. **Stay Safe:** You're critical to the group - don't take risks
4. **Prioritize Bandages:** Keep healing items flowing
5. **Blessed Food:** Give to Guardsmen and Miners who face danger
