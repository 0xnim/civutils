# Farmer Guide

Tips and strategies for leveling and playing as a [Farmer](farmer).

## Tips

1. **Sugarcane First:** Healers need it urgently for bandages
2. **Cows Priority:** Leather is a major bottleneck - start breeding early
3. **Wait to Harvest:** Don't harvest until level 2 for sustainable yields
4. **Alternate Rows:** Plant different crops in alternating rows for faster growth
5. **Berry Farms:** Great early food source that's fast to harvest

## Farm Designs

### Sweet Berries
Early game, walk into berry bushes and spam right-click. Build on moss for cheap material.

### Wheat/Potato/Carrot
9x9 farmland with water in the center (waterlogged leaves/slabs). Light with pumpkin lanterns. Alternate crops in rows.

### Sugarcane
Build in water bodies. Each block needs adjacent water. Cover water with slabs/leaves.

### Animals
Many small pens (2 animals each) is more efficient than large pens. Keep grass for cows/sheep to eat.
