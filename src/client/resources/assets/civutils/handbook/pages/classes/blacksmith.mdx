# Blacksmith

The Blacksmith's role is to craft upgraded tools and armor for players. Any player can craft wooden tools, but only a Blacksmith can craft tools made of better materials. See the [Smithing System](smithing) for the full crafting process.

## Gaining XP

### Crafting XP
| Item | XP per Material | Level Required |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| Wood Tools | 1 per plank | Novice (0) |
| Stone Tools | 2 per stone | Apprentice (1) |
| Leather Armor | 4 per leather | Apprentice (1) |
| Iron Equipment | 4 per iron | Journeyman (2) |
| Gold Equipment | 6 per gold | Expert (3) |
| Diamond Equipment | 8 per diamond | Master (4) |
| Arrows | 4 | Novice (0) |
| Crossbow | 6 | Journeyman (2) |

## Class Interactions

- **[Miners](miner)** supply stone, coal, and ores
- **[Guardsmen](guardsman)** rely heavily on Blacksmith equipment
- **[Librarians](librarian)** create blueprints needed for iron+ gear
