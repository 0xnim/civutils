# Farmer

The Farmer's main role is food production, which is vital especially in early game. Farmers are the only ones able to craft food items like bread, and they have higher crop harvest success rates. They are also the only class that can breed animals.

## Primary Abilities

### Harvest Success
Farmers have increasing chances of successfully harvesting crops:

| Level | Success Rate |
|-------|--------------|
| Novice (0) | 5% |
| Apprentice (1) | 10% |
| Journeyman (2) | 20% |
| Expert (3) | 40% |
| Master (4) | 70% |
| Grandmaster (5) | 90% |

### Animal Breeding
Only Farmers (level 2+) can breed animals. Breeding doesn't always succeed, but the chance increases with level.

**Important:** Breeding cost and cooldown scale with the number of animals in a pen. Build many small pens (2 animals each) for efficiency.

**Grass Feeding:** Cows, sheep, pigs, and chickens can eat grass (turning it to rooted dirt), which can trigger breeding without player action. Till or bonemeal rooted dirt to restore grass.

See [Animal Husbandry](animal-husbandry) for full details on taming and breeding mechanics.

### Smokers
Farmers can use smokers to create [smoked food](food-expiration), which lasts longer before expiring.

## Soil Quality System

Different biomes have different growth speeds for each crop:

| Tier | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Ungrowable | Crops turn to dead bushes |
| Very Slow | Functional but slow |
| Slow | Moderate speed |
| Good | Normal speed |
| Optimal | Fastest growth |

After farming 750 of a crop, hold that crop to see the soil quality for your current location.

## Class Interactions
