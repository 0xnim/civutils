# Farmer

The Farmer's role is food production, which is vital especially in early game. Farmers are the only ones able to craft food items like bread, and they have higher crop harvest success rates. They are also the only class that can breed animals.

## Harvest Success

| Level | Success Rate |
|-------|--------------|
| Novice (0) | 5% |
| Apprentice (1) | 10% |
| Journeyman (2) | 20% |
| Expert (3) | 40% |
| Master (4) | 70% |
| Grandmaster (5) | 90% |
