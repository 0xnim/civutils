# Class Levels

Each class has separate XP. Players begin with 0 XP in all classes. XP can be increased by performing class-related activities. **Death resets all XP in all classes to 0.**

## Level Requirements

Classes have 5 levels. Your level in a class depends on both a **minimum XP threshold** and what **percentage of your total XP** is in that class.

| Level | Name | Min XP | % of Total | XP from Previous |
|-------|------|--------|------------|------------------|
| 1 | Apprentice | 640 | 40% | 640 |
| 2 | Journeyman | 2,221 | 60% | 1,581 |
| 3 | Expert | 5,962 | 75% | 3,741 |
| 4 | Master | 14,852 | 85% | 8,890 |
| 5 | Grandmaster | 36,209 | 95% | 21,357 |
