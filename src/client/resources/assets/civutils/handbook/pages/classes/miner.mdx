# Miner

The Miner's role is to extract ores that [Blacksmiths](blacksmith) use to craft better tools and armor. Only high-level Miners can mine better ores (without the required level, there's only a ~1/7 chance to successfully harvest).

## Haste Bonuses

| Level | Haste |
|-------|-------|
| Apprentice (1) | Haste II |
| Journeyman (2) | Haste III |
| Expert (3) | Haste IV |
| Master (4) | Haste V |
| Grandmaster (5) | Haste VI |

## Gaining XP

| Block | XP | Level to Collect |
|-------|-----|------------------|
| Stone | 1 | Novice (0) |
| Coal | 4 | Novice (0) |
| Copper | 7 | Apprentice (1) |
| Deepslate | 2 | Apprentice (1) |
| Iron | 10 | Journeyman (2) |
| Redstone | 18 | Expert (3) |
| Lapis Lazuli | 18 | Expert (3) |
| Gold | 21 | Expert (3) |
| Diamond | 30 | Master (4) |
| Ancient Debris | 100 | Grandmaster (5) |

## Class Interactions

- **[Blacksmiths](blacksmith)** turn your ores into tools and equipment
- **[Healers](healer)** provide blessed food for dangerous expeditions
- **[Guardsmen](guardsman)** can escort you during cave trips
