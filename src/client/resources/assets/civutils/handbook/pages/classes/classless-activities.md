# Classless Activities

While many activities are tied to a class, there are helpful tasks that don't require any class level and don't give Class XP. Anyone can do these without hindering their leveling progress.

## Chopping Wood

The easiest and most useful classless activity. Wood is used for:
- Building
- Leveling [Blacksmiths](blacksmith) (smelting into charcoal)
- Leveling [Librarians](librarian) (bookshelves need planks)

Set up a small tree farm and everyone can help gather.

## Fishing

The easiest way to get food early game. Benefits:
- No class requirement
- Can fish up enchanted books
- Only needs string for a rod

**Tip:** To get string early, lure spiders into water and punch them from below. Use boats to escape drowneds. (Note: killing mobs gives some Guardsman XP)

## Crafting and Placing Torches

[Blacksmiths](blacksmith) often have extra charcoal from smelting logs. Use it to make torches and light up your surroundings - this significantly reduces mob spawns at night.

## Mining Shovel Blocks

Dirt, sand, and gravel don't require any class:
- **Dirt** - useful for farms
- **Sand** - for glass and TNT
- **Gravel** - for concrete or flint

## Mining Decorative Stone

These stones **don't give Miner XP** when mined:
- Diorite
- Andesite
- Granite

Anyone can collect these for building or help clear areas.

## Storage Sorting

A well-organized storage helps everyone. While Blacksmiths often manage storage, anyone can help sort and organize.

---

## Why This Matters

Since classes require a percentage of your total XP, gaining XP in one class makes it harder to level others. These classless activities let you contribute without diluting your specialization.
