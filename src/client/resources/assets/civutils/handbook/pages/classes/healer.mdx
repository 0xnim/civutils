# Healer

The Healer's role is providing healing to other players. With **natural regeneration disabled**, Healers are the only source of healing through bandages and blessed food. They can also revive downed players and eventually brew potions.

## Gaining XP

| Action | XP | Level Required |
|--------|-----|----------------|
| Crafting Hearty Soup | 50 | Novice (0) |
| Crafting Paper | 9 | Novice (0) |
| Crafting Bandages | 3 | Apprentice (1) |
| Blessing Food | 3 | Journeyman (2) |
| Crafting Blaze Rods | 45 | Master (4) |
| Crafting Nether Wart | 90 | Grandmaster (5) |
| Bandaging Player | 15-50 (scales) | Apprentice (1) |

## Class Interactions

- **[Farmers](farmer)** provide sugarcane and food for healing items
- **[Librarians](librarian)** need paper (Healers craft it for XP)
- **[Guardsmen](guardsman)** need constant healing support in combat

## Tips

1. **Get Sugarcane First:** You need ~4 stacks to reach level 1
2. **Consider Farmer First:** Level Farmer to gather sugarcane reliably, then death reset
3. **Stay Safe:** You're critical to the group - don't take risks
