# Guardsman Guide

Tips and strategies for leveling and playing as a [Guardsman](guardsman).

## Early Game

1. Kill passive mobs for low-risk XP (low gain but safe)
2. Attack mobs from underwater - most can't hit back
3. Beware skeletons, trident drowneds, and creepers
4. Use boats to trap hostile mobs for easy kills

## Mid Game

1. Work with [Builders](builder) to create mob farms
2. Protect Miners during cave expeditions
3. Always have [blessed food](healing-items) or bandages

## Combat Tips

- Guardsmen should always have the best equipment
- Stay with your Healer during dangerous operations
- Use your mob radar to track nearby threats
- Consider trapping mobs in boats (keep 3+ boats handy)
