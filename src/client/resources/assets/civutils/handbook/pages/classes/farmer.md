# Farmer

The Farmer's main role is food production, which is vital especially in early game. Farmers are the only ones able to craft food items like bread, and they have higher crop harvest success rates. They are also the only class that can breed animals.

## Primary Abilities

### Harvest Success
Farmers have increasing chances of successfully harvesting crops:

| Level | Success Rate |
|-------|--------------|
| Novice (0) | 5% |
| Apprentice (1) | 10% |
| Journeyman (2) | 20% |
| Expert (3) | 40% |
| Master (4) | 70% |
| Grandmaster (5) | 90% |

### Animal Breeding
Only Farmers (level 2+) can breed animals. Breeding doesn't always succeed, but the chance increases with level.

**Important:** Breeding cost and cooldown scale with the number of animals in a pen. Build many small pens (2 animals each) for efficiency.

### Smokers
Farmers can use smokers to create [smoked food](food-expiration), which lasts longer before expiring.

## Unlocks by Level

### Apprentice (1)
- 10% harvest success
- Crafting bread, cookies
- Crafting beetroot soup, mushroom soup
- Composters
- Using Smokers

### Journeyman (2)
- 20% harvest success
- Crafting pumpkin pie
- Animal breeding and taming (except dogs)
- Safe sweet berry harvesting

### Expert (3)
- 40% harvest success
- Crafting leads

### Master (4)
- 70% harvest success
- Crafting golden carrots

### Grandmaster (5)
- 90% harvest success

## Soil Quality System

Different biomes have different growth speeds for each crop:

| Tier | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Ungrowable | Crops turn to dead bushes |
| Very Slow | Functional but slow |
| Slow | Moderate speed |
| Good | Normal speed |
| Optimal | Fastest growth |

After farming 750 of a crop, hold that crop to see the soil quality for your current location.

## Class Interactions

- **[Healers](healer)** need sugarcane and food for healing items
- **[Librarians](librarian)** need leather for books
- **[Blacksmiths](blacksmith)** need leather for armor padding

## Tips

1. **Sugarcane First:** Healers need it urgently for bandages
2. **Cows Priority:** Leather is a major bottleneck - start breeding early
3. **Wait to Harvest:** Don't harvest until level 2 for sustainable yields
4. **Alternate Rows:** Plant different crops in alternating rows for faster growth
5. **Berry Farms:** Great early food source that's fast to harvest

## Farm Designs

### Sweet Berries
Early game, walk into berry bushes and spam right-click. Build on moss for cheap material.

### Wheat/Potato/Carrot
9x9 farmland with water in the center (waterlogged leaves/slabs). Light with pumpkin lanterns. Alternate crops in rows.

### Sugarcane
Build in water bodies. Each block needs adjacent water. Cover water with slabs/leaves.

### Animals
Many small pens (2 animals each) is more efficient than large pens. Keep grass for cows/sheep to eat.
