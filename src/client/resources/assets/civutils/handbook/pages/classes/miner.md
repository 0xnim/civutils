# Miner

The Miner's main role is to extract ores that [Blacksmiths](blacksmith) use to craft better tools and armor. Only high-level Miners can mine better ores. Miners gain permanent Haste bonuses as they level up.

## Primary Abilities

### Ore Mining
Miners progressively unlock the ability to mine higher-tier ores. Without the required level, there's only a ~1/7 chance to successfully harvest an ore.

### Haste Bonuses
Miners gain permanent Haste effects:

| Level | Haste |
|-------|-------|
| Apprentice (1) | Haste II |
| Journeyman (2) | Haste III |
| Expert (3) | Haste IV |
| Master (4) | Haste V |
| Grandmaster (5) | Haste VI |

## Class Interactions

- **[Blacksmiths](blacksmith)** turn your ores into tools and equipment
- **[Healers](healer)** provide blessed food for dangerous expeditions
- **[Guardsmen](guardsman)** can escort you during cave trips

## Mining Strategies

### Strip Mining
Mine at bedrock level for the best diamond/redstone/lapis yields. Deepslate gives 2 XP per block even at level 0.

### Water Mining
Swim through flooded caves to find ores safely. Place doors for air pockets. Watch for drowneds.

### Caving
Covers ground quickly but is dangerous. Bring a Guardsman and avoid night when mobs deal extra damage.

## Tips

1. **Track Time:** Use a clock - mobs are much stronger at night
2. **Mark Ore Veins:** If underleveled, mark locations to return later
3. **Save for Fortune:** Store ore blocks for Fortune-enchanted pickaxe later
4. **Surface Boulders:** Great early XP source
5. **Biome Matters:** Different biomes have different ore distributions
6. **TNT Mining:** Level 3 Miners can blast mine for 100% drops
7. **Creeper Trick:** Creepers have 1/3 chance to harvest exploded ores
8. **XP on Fail:** You still get XP even when failing to harvest

### Relog Trick
If surrounded by mobs with no players nearby, relogging will despawn them.
