# Force Feed

Force Feed is a mechanic that allows certain classes to feed other players directly. This is essential for keeping teammates fed during combat or when they can't eat themselves (such as when downed).

## How It Works

Hold food in your hand and right-click on another player to feed them. The food is consumed from your inventory and the target receives the healing/hunger benefits.

## Class Abilities

### Guardsman (Level 1)

Guardsmen can force feed players at **full food value**. This makes them excellent field support, keeping teammates fed during extended combat without any efficiency loss.

### Healer (Level 3)

Healers can force feed players at **half food value** (minimum half a shank). While less efficient than Guardsman feeding, this complements their other healing abilities and lets them provide emergency food to downed players.

## Tips

- **Prioritize downed players** - Force feeding can help revive teammates faster when combined with healing
- **Use high-value food** - Since you're spending hunger/food either way, use the best available
- **Coordinate with your team** - Call out when you're feeding someone so they don't waste their own food
- **Guardsmen are more efficient** - If both classes are available, have the Guardsman do the feeding
