# Player Leashing

At Guardsman level 5, you gain the ability to leash downed players using leads, allowing you to restrain and transport them.

## How to Use

1. Craft or obtain a lead
2. Find a downed player
3. Right-click them with the lead to attach it
4. Walk to drag them along

## Effects on Leashed Players

- **Cannot move** on their own
- **Cannot attack** or use items
- **Cannot break blocks**
- Can be dragged to a new location
- Remain leashed until the lead is removed or breaks

## Use Cases

- Capturing enemy players for questioning
- Moving downed allies to safety for revival
- Transporting prisoners to secure locations
- Preventing enemies from being revived by their allies
