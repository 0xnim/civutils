# Awakened

Awakened is a passive saving throw ability for Guardsmen. When damage would bring you to 2 or fewer hearts, there's a chance to trigger Awakened, granting temporary absorption hearts.

> *"Awakened: Muscle memory floods back from battles never fought."*

## Effects by Level

| Level | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Apprentice (1) | Absorption 2 |
| Journeyman (2) | Absorption 2 |
| Expert (3) | Absorption 3 |

## How It Works

- Triggers automatically when taking lethal or near-lethal damage
- Not guaranteed to activate every time
- Absorption hearts absorb damage before your real health
- Can save you from what would otherwise be a killing blow

## Tips

- Don't rely on Awakened alone - it's a backup, not a strategy
- Works well with [Healer](healer) support for follow-up healing
- Higher levels provide more absorption, increasing survival chance
