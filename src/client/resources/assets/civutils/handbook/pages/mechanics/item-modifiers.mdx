# Item Modifiers

Equipment crafted by Blacksmiths can have random bonus modifiers that improve their stats.

## Types of Modifiers

### Weapon Modifiers
- Bonus damage on critical hits
- Increased attack speed
- Additional knockback

### Armor Modifiers
- Reduced weight (less slowdown)
- Increased toughness
- Bonus protection

### Tool Modifiers
- Increased efficiency
- Better durability
- Fortune bonuses

## How to Get Modifiers

- Modifiers are randomly applied when a Blacksmith crafts equipment
- Higher-level Blacksmiths have better chances for good modifiers
- Quality of materials may affect modifier chances

## Viewing Modifiers

Check the item tooltip to see any modifiers present. They appear below the base stats.

## Tips

- Check tooltips before choosing equipment
- Good modifiers can make lower-tier gear competitive
- Modifiers persist through [repairs](repairing)
