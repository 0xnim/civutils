# Bandages

Bandages are the primary healing tool for Healers. They can heal injured players and revive downed ones.

## Crafting

**Recipe:** 8 Paper + 1 Sugar Cane → Bandage

**Costs:**
- 2 hunger to craft
- 5 hunger to use

## Healing

Right-click an injured player to heal them. The healing amount scales with Healer level:

| Level | Healing |
|-------|---------|
| Apprentice (1) | Base healing |
| Journeyman (2) | Increased |
| Expert (3) | Increased |
| Master (4) | Increased |
| Grandmaster (5) | Maximum |

## Revival

When a player is downed, use a bandage on them to begin the revival minigame:

1. A screen opens showing red and green items
2. Click red items to turn them green
3. When all items are green, the player is revived

## Tips

- Keep a stack of bandages at all times
- Pair with [Blessed Food](blessed-food) for sustained healing
- Healers gain XP from both crafting and using bandages
- Paper crafting also gives Healer XP - make your own supplies
