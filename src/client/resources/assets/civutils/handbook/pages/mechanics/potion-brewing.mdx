# Potion Brewing

High-level Healers unlock the ability to craft the ingredients needed for brewing potions, which are otherwise unobtainable.

## Blaze Rods (Level 4)

**Recipe:**
- 1 Warped Nylium
- 1 Crimson Nylium
- 1 Gold Ingot
- 3 Gunpowder

Blaze Rods are used to:
- Craft Blaze Powder (fuel for brewing stands)
- Craft [Brewing Stands](item:brewing_stand)

## Nether Wart (Level 5)

**Recipe:**
- 1 Beetroot
- 3 Coarse Dirt
- 1 Blaze Powder

Nether Wart is the base ingredient for most potions:
- Awkward Potion (base for most brewing)
- Can be farmed once obtained

## Brewing Stand Usage

Requires Healer level 4 to craft and interact with. See [Brewing Stand](item:brewing_stand) for details.

## Tips

- Crafting Blaze Rods gives 45 Healer XP
- Crafting Nether Wart gives 90 Healer XP
- Consider stockpiling Nether Wart to grow your own farm
