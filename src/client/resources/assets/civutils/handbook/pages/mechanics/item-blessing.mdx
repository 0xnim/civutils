# Item Blessing

Librarians level 2+ can bless items to give them random enchantments using books.

## How to Bless Items

1. Hold a **book** in your off-hand
2. Hold the item to enchant in your main hand
3. Shift + right-click

## Requirements

- Consumes the book
- Costs Minecraft XP levels equal to your Librarian level
- The enchantment is random

## Important Notes

- The enchantment may not be compatible with the item
- Multiple blessings can stack enchantments
- Higher XP cost = potentially better enchantments
- Works on tools, armor, and weapons

## Tips

- Save XP for important equipment
- Bless items for [Guardsmen](guardsman) and [Miners](miner) first
- Incompatible enchantments are wasted - know what can go on what
- Books are cheap to craft and give Librarian XP
