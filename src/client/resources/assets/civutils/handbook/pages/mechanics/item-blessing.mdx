# Item Blessing

Librarians level 2+ can bless items to give them random enchantments using books.

## How to Bless Items

1. Hold a **book** in your off-hand
2. Hold the item to enchant in your main hand
3. Shift + right-click

## Requirements

- Consumes the book
- Costs Minecraft XP levels equal to your Librarian level
- The enchantment is random

## Important Notes

- **An item can only be blessed once**
- The enchantment is random from the full enchantment pool (including treasure enchants like Mending and curses)
- The enchantment may not be compatible with the item (e.g., Smite on a fishing rod) - this wastes the blessing
- Blessed items display the blessing and who blessed it when you hover over them

## Tips

- Save XP for important equipment
- Bless items for [Guardsmen](guardsman) and [Miners](miner) first
- Know which enchantments are valid for each item type to avoid wasted blessings
- Books are cheap to craft and give Librarian XP
