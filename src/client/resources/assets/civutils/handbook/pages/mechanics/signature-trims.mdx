# Signature Armor Trims

Every Blacksmith has a unique armor trim pattern and color assigned based on their username. When they craft armor, there's a chance their signature appears.

## How It Works

- Each Blacksmith's name generates a unique pattern + color combination
- When crafting armor, there's a chance the signature trim appears
- Signed armor also shows a tag with the crafter's name

## Benefits

- **Identification** - Know who made your gear
- **Prestige** - Master Blacksmiths become known for their work
- **Aesthetics** - Unique look for your civilization's armor

## Viewing Signatures

Check the item tooltip to see:
- The armor trim pattern and color
- The Blacksmith who crafted it
- Any [Item Modifiers](item-modifiers) present

## Tips

- High-level Blacksmiths have better signature chances
- Collect armor from different Blacksmiths for variety
- Signature doesn't affect stats - purely cosmetic
