# Interdimensional Travel

In CivLabs, players can travel between different worlds using the Nether as a hub.

## Homeworlds

When you first join an event, you're randomly assigned to a **Homeworld**. This is:
- The world you start in
- Your default respawn location (if no bed is set)

## Nether Changes

The Nether works differently in CivLabs:

- **Shared Nether:** All worlds share the same Nether
- **1:1 Coordinates:** Nether coordinates match Overworld (no fast travel)
- **No Entity Travel:** Entities (mobs, animals) cannot travel through portals

## World Portals

Nether portals have special **corner blocks** that determine their destination:

- Each world has a unique corner block type
- Portals in the Nether with valid corner blocks go to that world
- Generic portals (no special corners) send you to your Homeworld
- Invalid corner blocks also default to Homeworld

### How to Travel

1. Enter the Nether from any world
2. Find or build a portal with the destination world's corner blocks
3. Enter the portal to travel to that world

### Discovering Corner Blocks

Corner block types must be discovered through in-game means. Explore the Nether to find portals and identify which blocks correspond to which worlds.

## Travel Rules

- **No Direct Travel:** You cannot go directly from one Overworld to another
- **Always Through Nether:** Travel path is: World A → Nether → World B
- **Progress Retained:** Your name, inventory, and class XP travel with you

## Respawning

Your respawn location depends on these factors (in order):

1. **Bed in Current World:** If you have a valid bed in your current world, respawn there
2. **Bed in Homeworld:** If you have a valid bed in your Homeworld, respawn there
3. **Homeworld Spawn:** Otherwise, respawn at your Homeworld's spawn point

**Note:** The Nether counts as its own "world" for respawn purposes.

## Strategic Considerations

### Exploration
- Scout other worlds for resources or allies
- Map portal locations in the Nether

### Defense
- Control Nether portals to your world
- Build defenses around your world's entry points

### Trade
- Travel between worlds for trading
- Establish cross-world alliances

### Emergencies
- Know where portals are if you need to escape
- Keep backup gear in other worlds
