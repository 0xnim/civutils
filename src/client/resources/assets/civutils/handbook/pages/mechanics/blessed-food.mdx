# Blessed Food

Healers level 2+ can bless food to give it regeneration and absorption effects when eaten.

## How to Bless Food

1. Hold food in your hand
2. Shift + right-click
3. The food becomes blessed (costs 10 hunger)

## Effects by Level

| Level | Regeneration | Absorption |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| Journeyman (2) | 5s Regen I | 5s Absorption I |
| Expert (3) | 10s Regen I | 10s Absorption I |
| Master (4) | 15s Regen I | 15s Absorption I |
| Grandmaster (5) | 20s Regen I | 20s Absorption I |

## Additional Benefits

- Blessed food lasts longer before [expiring](food-expiration)
- Stacks with the food's normal hunger restoration
- Can be given to other players

## Tips

- Bless high-value foods for maximum efficiency
- Give blessed food to [Guardsmen](guardsman) and [Miners](miner) heading into danger
- Blessing food gives 3 Healer XP
- Combine with [smoked food](item:smoker) for longest-lasting supplies
