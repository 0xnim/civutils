# Repairing

Blacksmiths level 3+ can repair tools and armor directly on an anvil without using materials.

## How to Repair

1. Hold the damaged item in your hand
2. Look at an [Anvil](item:anvil)
3. Shift + right-click

## Costs

- **Minecraft XP** - Amount scales with damage and item tier
- **Hunger** - Repairing consumes hunger

## Stat Decay

Repaired items may suffer from stat decay over time. To restore stats:
- Use a [Whetstone](item:whetstone) on the item
- This restores the item's original stats

## Repair vs Replace

Consider whether repairing or crafting new is more efficient:
- Repairing saves materials but costs XP
- High-tier items are usually worth repairing
- Low-tier items may be cheaper to replace

## Tips

- Keep XP ready for emergency repairs
- Repair before items break completely
- Use Whetstones regularly on important gear
