# Pick-Up

At Healer level 3+, you can pick up downed players to carry them to safety or access their inventory.

## How to Use

### Carrying
1. Approach a downed player (without bandages in hand)
2. Right-click to pick them up
3. Walk to move them with you
4. Right-click again to set them down

### Inventory Access
1. Approach a downed player
2. Shift + right-click to open their inventory
3. You can move items in/out of their inventory

## Use Cases

- Move downed allies away from danger before reviving
- Access a downed player's supplies in emergencies
- Transport players to a safer revival location
- Retrieve important items from fallen teammates
