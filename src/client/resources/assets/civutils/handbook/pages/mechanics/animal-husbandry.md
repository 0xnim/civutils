# Animal Husbandry

Animal taming and breeding has been significantly changed in CivLabs. Different animals require different classes to tame, and breeding is more complex with costs that scale based on pen population.

## Taming Animals

### Phantoms
Phantoms can be tamed by feeding them **clocks** - roughly 32 are needed. Once tamed:
- They grow in size and can be mounted
- When mounted, they fly where your crosshair points (slightly faster than a happy ghast)
- When dismounted, they stay in place and won't fly away

### Horses
Only **[Farmers](farmer) level 2+** can tame horses, but anyone can ride them once tamed.

### Dogs (Wolves)
Only **[Guardsmen](guardsman) level 1+** can tame wolves into dogs. Dogs remain tamed regardless of what class you switch to. However, **breeding dogs requires a Farmer**, so building a dog army requires coordination between Guardsman and Farmer.

### Cats
Cats can be crafted using a Cat Spawn Egg:
- 1 Egg + 1 Diamond + 5 Tropical Fish

## Breeding Animals

### Pen Size Mechanics
Breeding cost and cooldown **scale with the number of animals in a pen**. A pen is defined as an area enclosed by fences.

| More Animals | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| Higher food cost | More food items needed per breeding attempt |
| Longer cooldown | More time between breedings |

**Optimal Strategy:** Build many small pens with 2 animals each, rather than one large pen.

### Grass Eating
Cows, sheep, pigs, and chickens can **eat grass**, which:
- Turns the grass block into **rooted dirt**
- Can trigger breeding without player action
- Reduces the food you need to provide

**Restoring Grass:**
- Bonemeal rooted/coarse dirt to turn it back to regular dirt
- Tilling rooted/coarse dirt also works (cheaper)
- Grass will then spread back naturally

### Which Animals Are Affected
The pen size mechanics apply to:
- Cows
- Sheep
- Pigs
- Chickens

**Bees are NOT affected** by these changes and breed normally.

## Pen Design Tips

### Small Isolated Pens (2 animals each)
Most efficient for breeding:
- Lowest food cost
- Shortest breeding cooldown
- Kill one animal after each successful breeding

### Medium Pens (3x3 or 4x4)
Good balance if you want grass feeding:
- Animals can eat grass and breed passively
- Still manageable breeding costs
- Keep one pair per pen

### Checking Breeding Status
When attempting to feed an animal for breeding:
- Food required appears above the hotbar
- Breeding cooldown (if active) also displays
- Make sure both animals have no cooldown and you have enough food

## Class Requirements Summary

| Animal | Taming | Breeding |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Phantoms | Anyone (32 clocks) | N/A |
| Horses | Farmer (2) | Farmer (2) |
| Dogs | Guardsman (1) | Farmer (2) |
| Cats | Anyone (spawn egg) | Farmer (2) |
| Cows/Sheep/Pigs/Chickens | Farmer (2) | Farmer (2) |
| Bees | Anyone | Anyone |
