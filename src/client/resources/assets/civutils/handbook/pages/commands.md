# Commands

This page lists the important commands available in CivLabs.

## Essential Commands

### /class
Opens the class menu showing:
- Your current class XP in all classes
- Available unlocks for each class
- Your in-game name

**This is the most important command to remember.**

## Character Setup

### /namechoice, /nameoptions, /setname
Used in the first 10 minutes of play to choose your name from the given options. You can also click a name in chat.

### /chatbubble set [options]
Customize your chat bubble:
- Color
- Volume
- Opener/closer style

*Note: May only work in the lobby.*

## Utility Commands

### /sit
Sit on the block you're standing on.
- **Removes idle hunger drain** while sitting
- Good for conserving food during downtime

### /cannonball
Launches you up and forward. Fun movement ability.

### /emotes
Opens a menu of cosmetic emote actions.

## Death Commands

When you are **downed** (not fully dead), these commands will finish you off:

- `/suicide`
- `/die`
- `/giveup`
- `/gg`
- `/rip`
- `/d`

Use these when you're downed with no chance of revival.

## Tips

1. **Check /class regularly:** Monitor your progress and unlocks
2. **Use /sit:** Great for AFK moments to save hunger
3. **Set your name early:** You only have 10 minutes to choose
4. **Don't /die too quickly:** Wait to see if a [Healer](healer) can revive you
